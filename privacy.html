DPEA Portfolios — Privacy

The short version

DPEA Portfolios is a set of web tools made by DPEA teachers for DPEA students. Everything runs in your browser. We have no server and no database, and we don't collect, store or sell any of your data.

Without signing in

Your name, student ID, grade, rotation or period, and your drafts are saved only in your own browser on your own device. Posts you make download to your device, and you choose where to submit them (Canvas). Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, the tools save copies of your work to your own Google Drive, in a folder called DPEA Portfolio Data.

  • We ask Google for one permission only: drive.file. It lets this site see and change only the files and folders it created. It cannot see anything else in your Drive, your email, your calendar or your Classroom work.
  • We also read your Google account's email address (to show you which account is signed in) and how full your Drive is (to warn you before it runs out of space).
  • Your files go straight from your browser to Google. They never pass through a DPEA server, and teachers can't see them unless you share them.
  • The sign-in only lasts while you're using the site. Nothing is kept on our side.

To stop, choose Sign out on the home page. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your folder and files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

DPEA Portfolios' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
