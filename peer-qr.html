Scan to open your peer review

First time on this iPad?
Scan this to sign in with Clever
Open Canvas from Clever once, then scan the big code.
  1. Scan with your iPad camera
    It opens the assignment in Safari, not the Canvas app. Sign in to Canvas if it asks.
  2. Tap your classmate under Peer Reviews
    Download the .html file they submitted.
  3. Open the Peer Review tool
    Go to portfolio.dpeacl.org, tap Peer Review, and drop in the file. Paste your review into a Canvas comment.
