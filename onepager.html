How this works: a one-pager introduces your exhibit to someone walking up to it. Explain the science first, then the exhibit — each with one or two figures. Everything has to fit on a single page, so the preview tells you if you've written too much.
The figures grow or shrink to fill whatever room the writing leaves. Refer to them in your text as "figure 1", "figure 2" and so on — the numbers are shown under each photo below.
Equations: type symbols straight in — θ, λ, Δ, ², ₁ — or copy them from a site like compart.com.
